## Visual review

- Beat order is visible and stable: screen proof → candidate comparison → process timeline → host reflection → chapter resolution.
- Screen proof includes context blur, focus frame and host inset rather than a raw full-frame screen dump.
- Candidate comparison exposes a selected take and visually de-emphasizes alternatives.
- Process timeline communicates the active workflow step with a readable horizontal relationship.
- Host reflection uses the amber line-art source, directional lighting and cinematic subtitle treatment.
- Chapter card provides a clean negative-space reset before the next beat.

## Known limitations

- Opening frame is intentionally near-black during the first entrance phase; the final editor should expose this hold and let the reviewer decide whether it is earned.
- Fixture reuses two source frames as temporary screen/host assets; it does not yet demonstrate agent-generated character consistency or a bespoke metaphor asset.
- Music is a plumbing fixture, not a final IsaacVerse music bed.
- Voice uses a short enhanced reference segment and does not yet demonstrate sentence-level multi-take chop-combine.
- No full-screen motion path or real screen-record capture is included in this pilot.
