# Video 02 — Script tutorial visual evidence

Source: `02 - How I Actually Write Viral Scripts.mp4` (1280x720, 60fps).
Contact sheets sampled at 15-second intervals across the video.

## Evidence from the sampled sequence

- The opening uses YouTube analytics/dashboard evidence, then moves into the recurring amber Isaac line-art world.
- A recurring visual language combines:
  - browser/document screenshots;
  - analytics graphs and retention curves;
  - dark floating cards with colored labels;
  - browser comments and video thumbnails;
  - movie/stock footage inserts;
  - amber line-art Isaac character shots;
  - minimal white/yellow typography cards on black;
  - diagrams made from nodes, lines, cards and labels.
- The **hero's journey is rendered as a circular visual system**: Isaac portrait in the center, amber nodes around a ring, and labeled points including status quo, call to adventure, departure, trials, approach, crisis, treasure, resolution and new life.
- Other narrative concepts are also made visible as diagrams: main problem/deeper problem, retention graph, research notes, feedback/refine paths, and connected-story maps.
- Screen captures are framed as evidence inside the story; they are not dumped as raw full-screen tutorials. The crop, label, surrounding black space, portrait/avatar and colored markers are coordinated.
- Text has multiple roles: large chapter/title cards, small subtitle text, diagram labels, source UI text, and highlighted keywords. These are not one generic text overlay.
- The recurring amber Isaac world acts as a continuity anchor, while each concept gets a different semantic visual construction. The visual system is consistent; the composition is content-specific.

## Important implication

The hero's journey is not only a script template. It has a **visual grammar**: a circular node map, labeled milestones, center character, and transitions between narrative states. A production agent needs a semantic mapping from `journey_slot` to a family of visual constructions, not a flat list of effects.

Example mapping evidence:

| Narrative meaning | Observed visual construction |
|---|---|
| Proof/analytics | Dashboard or graph with readable metric and controlled highlight |
| Story structure | Circular path with nodes and labeled milestones |
| Main vs deeper problem | Two-level typography/diagram with distinct emphasis colors |
| Research/outline | Browser/document screenshot staged inside black composition |
| Reflection/voice | Amber Isaac close-up with subtitle in letterbox |
| Example/story reference | Movie/stock insert framed as a narrative illustration |

This is a **shot/beat grammar**, not a reusable spatial layout catalog.
