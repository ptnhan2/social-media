# Video 07 — One-video growth visual evidence

Source: `07 - How I Broke Youtube with 1 Video.mp4` (1280x720, 60fps).
Contact sheet sampled at 20-second intervals.

## Evidence-backed construction

- Starts with a black stage, analytical metrics and Isaac identity, then moves into cinematic live-action examples and recurring amber line-art character shots.
- Hero's Journey is shown as a circular diagram with labeled nodes, then reused as a visual framework around film examples.
- YouTube channel pages, analytics graphs, thumbnails and comments are treated as proof objects and staged with the host.
- Large isolated words on black (`YOURSELF`, `BROAD`, etc.) punctuate the argument.
- The visual grammar cycles between: claim card → cinematic example → diagram → analytics proof → host/world. This is a narrative argument structure, not a layout loop.
