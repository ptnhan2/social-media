## Evidence-backed grammar

- **World style**: black cinematic field + warm amber line-art, not simply an overlay filter. The style is applied to environments, props, character and light sources as one coherent world.
- **Letterbox/subtitle system**: recurring black bars and italic warm-yellow subtitles at the bottom. This is a narrative delivery layer distinct from UI/source text.
- **Shot language**: prop establishing shot → desk detail → close-up → medium → graphic title/emphasis → extreme close-up. The sequence controls attention and emotional escalation.
- **Metaphor over interface**: books labeled Voice Mastery/Audio Engineering, keyboard, mug, eyes and face are used to externalize an invisible technical subject.
- **Accent logic**: cyan appears as a rare high-contrast emphasis color against the amber world, not as a default palette option on every element.
- **Do not flatten into atoms**: “gold glow” is not the reusable unit. The reusable unit is a coordinated **shot treatment**: subject/prop + camera scale + lighting motif + grade + subtitle role + transition/timing.
