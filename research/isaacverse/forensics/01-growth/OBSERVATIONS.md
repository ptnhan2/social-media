# Video 01 — Channel growth visual evidence

Source: `01 - How I Blew Up My Youtube Channel.mp4` (1280x720, 60fps).
Contact sheets sampled at 20-second intervals.

## Evidence-backed construction

- Opens with a black analytical stage showing subscriber count, video count and viewer comments as floating evidence cards.
- Uses recurring amber/gold Isaac line-art portraits and hand gestures as the host's visual identity.
- Alternates between cinematic live-action/movie inserts, YouTube channel pages, analytics dashboards, thumbnails, comment stacks, and stylized diagrams.
- Shows a red-vs-green comparison: boring edit/skippy animations/optimized script versus fun edit/clean animations/human script. The diagram is a visual argument, not decorative UI.
- Uses a back-catalog strip of thumbnails to make “format series” visible as a system.
- Uses classroom/lab/movie-like scenes and a low-poly mountain as metaphors for learning, journey and progress.
- Returns repeatedly to black + amber line-art Isaac shots with small subtitles, creating continuity between abstract argument sections.
- The same content is shown in multiple evidence modes: metrics, comments, dashboard, thumbnail wall, diagram, metaphor scene and character performance.

## Implication

The recurring reusable unit is a **proof/story transformation**: take an abstract claim (format, growth, transformation), choose the evidence mode that makes it emotionally and cognitively legible, then return to the host/world. This is a higher-order composition rule, not a fixed spatial layout or isolated visual effect.
