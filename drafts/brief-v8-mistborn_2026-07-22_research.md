## AI failure modes (all 7 dimensions)

| Dimension | Allomancy does | AI does |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Comprehension | 4×4 predictive grid | Random list of unrelated abilities |
| Limitations | Newton's Third Law applied | "Can control fire" with no physics |
| Cost | Metal scarcity = economic constraint | "Mana/energy drain" generic |
| Source | Genetics = class oppression | Magic "just exists" |
| Depth | Same metals, 3 different mechanics | Unrelated subsystems added ad hoc |
| Consistency | Pre-planned trilogy | Contradicts itself chapter to chapter |
| Visual | Blue lines = rule communication | Glowing effects, no mechanical meaning |

## 7 Prompt design principles for AI magic systems

1. Start with constraint, not power
2. Force a taxonomy (4×4 grid, not random list)
3. Encode social hierarchy into access
4. Apply Newton's Third Law to every ability
5. Design visual FROM mechanical (not aesthetic)
6. Plan expansion before writing first power
7. Make cost structural, not numerical
