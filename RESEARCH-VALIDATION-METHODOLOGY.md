## 1. Five-Stage Validation Process (Consensus)

### Stage 1: Search Demand Verification
- **YouTube Autocomplete**: Type topic into search bar. Real queries from real people. If it doesn't autocomplete, demand is questionable.
- **Wildcard trick**: Use `*` before or after keyword for variant discovery.
- **Alphabet trick**: Type `topic a`, `topic b`, `topic c` to find all autocomplete variants.
- **Keyword tools** (optional): vidIQ, TubeBuddy, Ahrefs YouTube Keyword Tool for search volume + competition data.

### Stage 2: Competitor Analysis
For each of 5-10 top YouTube search results, collect:
- Video title, views, **channel subscriber count**, upload date
- **Outlier score** = Video views ÷ Channel median views (use median, not average)
- Why it worked: format? title pattern? thumbnail? timing? personality? search demand?
- Content gap: what do they miss?

**Outlier score interpretation:**
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 2x | Worth noticing |
| 3-5x | Strong signal |
| 10x+ | Deep analysis warranted |

**Mid-sized channel test:**
- 10K-100K sub channels regularly hit 50K+ views → demand is real
- Only huge channels (500K+) get views → saturated

> "A 1M-view video on a 10M-sub channel is NOT an outlier. A 50K-view video on a 5K-sub channel IS a 10x outlier — this is the gold." — OverseerOS

> "Analyze channels 2-3x your size. These show what your realistic next level looks like. Mega-channels don't teach what works at your scale." — OutlierKit

### Stage 3: Google Trends — YouTube Search Mode
1. Go to Google Trends
2. **Switch from "Web Search" to "YouTube Search"** (critical — most people miss this)
3. Set timeframe to 5 years for seasonality + long-term trajectory
4. Compare 2-5 terms simultaneously (relative interest)
5. Check "Rising" related queries (percentage growth — early signal before peak)
6. Check "Top" related queries (most-searched adjacent topics)
7. Filter by country/region for localized demand

**What Google Trends tells you:** Rising/falling/flat trajectory, seasonality, term prioritization, gap detection (high interest + outdated videos = opportunity)
**What it does NOT tell you:** Absolute search volume (0-100 relative index only), competition difficulty, whether your channel can rank

### Stage 4: Content Gap Analysis
Four gap types:
| Gap Type | Signal | Best Move |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Missing question | Viewers repeatedly asking same thing in comments | Answer it directly |
| Weak comparison | Competitors cover partial market, not full decision | Create complete comparison |
| Audience segment | One viewer group consistently ignored | Target that subgroup |
| Format gap | Topic exists but not in useful format | Repackage in better format |

> "A gap does not always mean nobody has made a video on the topic. It can be a missing angle, format, audience segment, update, comparison, or proof." — OverseerOS

> Channels publishing on gaps earn 30-50% more views than competitive content. — TubeAnalytics

### Stage 5: Scorecard (OverseerOS, 10 categories, 0-50)

Score each 0-5:
| Category | Question |
|----------|----------|
| Viewer clarity | How specific is the target viewer? |
| Pain/desire strength | How urgent is the problem? |
| Demand evidence | Competitor breakouts, search data, comments, trends |
| Search potential | Search intent + volume match |
| Suggested/browse potential | Curiosity gap + visual click potential |
| Title potential | Can write 5-10 strong title options? |
| Thumbnail potential | Visual tension, mobile readability |
| Retention potential | Natural structure, payoff, rewatch hooks |
| Differentiation | Hard-to-copy angle |
| Channel fit | Pillar alignment, positioning |

| Score | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| 0-15 | Kill |
| 16-24 | Save for later |
| 25-32 | Needs sharper angle |
| 33-40 | Produce |
| 41-50 | Priority |

### Saturation Metrics

| Metric | Source | Threshold |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Mid-sized channel view test | vidIQ | 10K-100K subs hit 50K+ views = real demand |
| Saturation Score (0-100) | Pulsenaut | 0-30 Wide Open, 31-50 Opportunity, 51-70 Competitive, 71-100 Saturated |
| Niche Penetration % | AutonoLab | <10% blue ocean, 10-30% viable, 30-50% saturated, >50% dominated |
| Weighted Supply Score | AutonoLab | 1M+ sub channels = 10x weight, <10K = 1x |

**Niche Penetration formula (AutonoLab):**
```
Niche Penetration % = (Total subs of top 10 creators / Total addressable market) × 100
```

### Critical Insight
> "Competition proves there's an audience hungry for content. If no one else is making videos on your topic, it might mean there's simply no demand." — Subscribr

## 4. Outlier Analysis

### Definition
YouTube outlier analysis finds videos that performed much better than a channel's normal baseline. Compare each video against the channel's average or median performance.

### Formula
```
Outlier Score = Video Views ÷ Channel Baseline Views (median)
```

### Why It's Powerful
- Fixes the "MrBeast problem" — analyzing mega-channels teaches nothing about what works at your scale
- A 1M-view video on a 10M-sub channel is NOT an outlier (normal for them)
- A 50K-view video on a 5K-sub channel IS a 10x outlier — this shows what works at YOUR scale

### Expert Workflow (OverseerOS)
1. Build a list of 10-30 channels (5 large, 10 mid, 10 small with momentum)
2. Find videos with 3x+ channel baseline
3. Study: topic, packaging, timing, hook structure, audience desire, repeatability
4. Extract the pattern, don't copy the video

### Tools
- vidIQ Outliers Tool
- Viewstats (MrBeast's tool)
- Outlyr, OutlierKit, KeywordsRocket, Outlier Finder

## 5. Additional Frameworks

### Alan Spicer's 200 Video Rule
If you can't brainstorm 200 unique ideas in the niche, it's too narrow.

### MrBeast's Interest Market Cap
Combine multiple high-interest topics: hydraulic press (50M views/28 days) + Lamborghini (30M) → combined = 80M potential audience.

### Colin & Samir's Three Rules
1. If They Don't Click, They Don't Watch — title + thumbnail before video
2. Respect Their Time — hook (7-30s) must deliver on thumbnail promise
3. Do You Want More Stuff Like This? — CTAs + binge paths

### Think with Google: Help-Hub-Hero
- **Help**: How-to, search-driven, evergreen
- **Hub**: Regular scheduled content, builds audience habit
- **Hero**: Tentpole events, massive step-change in growth
