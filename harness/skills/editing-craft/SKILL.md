# Editing Craft

## Murch's Rule of Six (priority order)
1. Emotion (51%) — does the cut serve the emotional truth?
2. Story (23%) — does it advance the narrative?
3. Rhythm (10%) — is the pacing right?
4. Eye-trace (7%) — where is the viewer looking?
5. 2D plane (5%) — is the spatial relationship consistent?
6. 3D space (4%) — is the 3D continuity maintained?

## Pacing
- Cut on action, not on boredom
- Vary shot duration — don't let it become mechanical
- "Make the stop worth waiting for" — silence is a tool
- Beat-driven: cut on downbeat in montage, off-beat in dialogue

## Continuity
- 180 degree rule, eyeline match, match-on-action
- Focal point stability across cuts (rule of thirds preferred)
- 30 degree rule for camera angle changes

## Sound Design
- Whoosh = transition, Riser = build, Impact = reveal/emphasis
- "Mute then add" — feel the sound placement without VO+music
- Sound design is half the video
- No one-SFX-per-element — combine and layer

## Motion Discipline
- Effects complement the script — never max effects
- "Just because you can doesn't mean you should"
- Focus consistency: focal point stays in the same spot across cuts
