# Research Note: The Timeline Is Not The Edit

This acceptance project starts from a new argument rather than the old Composer fixture.

## Claim

Polish does not create meaning. A useful edit makes the decision visible, then uses motion and sound to help the viewer feel that decision.

## Evidence Plan

- Original control-room diagram: evidence must prove a decision.
- Original paper-signal diagram: a cut connects signal, choice, and meaning.
- Original noise-field metaphor: excess motion can hide the change.
- Original human-profile composition: the final rule must remain usable by a person.

## Production Boundary

The pilot uses local SVG graphics, Windows SAPI narration, and FFmpeg-generated audio. No paid provider is called implicitly.
